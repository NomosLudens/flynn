## 10.16 Next product task at the time — energy limits physical capacity

The homeostatic need was already real:

```text
neural_time ↑
→ energy_reserve ↓
→ feeding_drive ↑
```

The next step was not to create a food-search policy or reopen neural modulation. It was to implement a simple physical body consequence:

```text
requested actuation
× body_capacity(energy_reserve)
= executable actuation
```

Principles:

```text
ENERGY_MODULATES_ACTION_SELECTION=NO
FEEDING_DRIVE_SELECTS_ACTION=NO
ENERGY_MODULATES_PHYSICAL_CAPACITY=TARGET
ZERO_ENERGY_ACTIVE_ACTUATION=NO
BODY_STATE_RESET_AT_ZERO_ENERGY=NO
```

The first candidate curve was continuous and deterministic (`body_capacity = energy_reserve`) if compatible with runtime. Low energy reduces available force/speed/amplitude; zero energy eliminates new active actuation; energy recovery automatically restores capacity.

It also needed to be validated that `energy_reserve`, `feeding_drive`, Pinocchio `q`, position, and `body_capacity` survive a controlled restart without reverting to defaults.

Epistemology is executed inside the work: do, observe, validate, and close. It should not become a sequence of processes that substitutes for resident life.

# 14. OPERATIONAL FAILURES AND DIRECTION CORRECTIONS

This section records not only runtime failures, but also failures of **orchestration/assistance** that negatively altered the journey. They are part of project history and should not be erased.

## 14.1 Excessive micro-processes and gates

There was drift in which epistemic rigor was confused with process proliferation: audits of audits, successive continuations, remappings, very long prompts, and sub-gates for questions already answered. This consumed time/quota and moved development away from the real product.

Correction:

```text
EPISTEMIC_RULE=
IMPLEMENT -> OBSERVE_REAL_RESULT -> VALIDATE -> CLOSE_OR_FIX_REAL_BLOCKER
```

Tests and ablations remain valid, but they are internal to the work and should not automatically create new projects.

## 14.2 Resident product was temporarily subordinated to shadow/replay

Development entered a period in which checkpoint, shadow, replay, reset, and controlled repetition received more weight than resident trajectory. This produced **reliable repetition**, but not the persistent consequence that defines the intended product.

Conceptual failure:

```text
checkpoint -> run -> measure -> reset -> repeat
```

is not equivalent to:

```text
Flynn(t0) -> consequence -> Flynn(t1) -> consequence -> Flynn(t2)
```

Consolidated correction:

```text
CAVERNA=RESIDENT_LIFE
LAB=REPLAY_AND_ANALYSIS
LIVE_BODY_DRIVEN_BY_REPLAY=NO
BODY_STATE_RESET_AFTER_VALIDATION=NO
TRAJECTORY_RESET=NO
```

## 14.3 Improper reopening of already canonical decisions

Assistance repeatedly treated author decisions as open questions: it attempted to reopen morphology, propose a minimal/3DoF/4DoF body, and derive the body from a MotorVector even though the canonical body was already the Lethe sigil.

Correction:

```text
AUTHORITATIVE_BODY=CURRENT_LETHE_SIGIL
SIGIL_MODULES=23
SIGIL_CONNECTIONS=30
```

Pinocchio must serve the existing sigil; the sigil should not be redesigned to serve a convenient model.

## 14.4 Excessive blocking due to absence of MotorVector

After E01-N demonstrated `REAL_NEURAL_MOTOR_VECTOR=NO` and `VNC_BOUNDARY_SUPPORTED=YES`, assistance initially insisted on keeping E02 blocked and searching for another motor representation. That prolonged the same problem without changing available information.

Correction: preserve the negative scientific result and advance through explicit architectural decision:

```text
BIOLOGICAL_VNC_RECONSTRUCTED=NO
SYNTHETIC_MOTOR_BOUNDARY=YES
```

Subsequent integration proved in the real product `FLYNN_CONNECTED_TO_PINOCCHIO=YES` without rewriting E01-N's scientific result.

## 14.5 Improper universal formulations

In earlier stages, some E01-D conclusions were stated too universally (`COORDINATION_LAYER_REQUIRED=NO`, `ZEROCLAW_MOTOR_COORDINATION_REQUIRED=NO`). The real support was scoped to the tested protocol with `TEST_HARNESS_GENERATED`.

Correct form:

```text
ZEROCLAW_MOTOR_COORDINATION_REQUIRED_FOR_TESTED_E01_D_TASK=NO
```

The actual product advanced through different evidence: real resident integration with a deterministic synthetic boundary.

## 14.6 MAX incident and expensive operational choice

Attempting to compile the Rust/ZeroClaw surface on MAX caused strong memory pressure and perceived host unavailability. Later forensics found `MEMORY_PRESSURE_EVIDENCE=YES`, correlation with `cargo/rustc`, and no proof of kernel OOM. Insisting on a monolithic surface for a small transformation was a poor operational choice.

Correction: do not repeat a heavy build on MAX without structural necessity. The historical architecture did not require ZeroClaw as motor coordinator.

## 14.7 False fallback in the homeostasis UI

The UI could invent `100%` energy and `0.00` feeding drive when data were absent. Although not triggered during the continuity audit, the fallback violated the principle of not fabricating state.

H01 correction:

```text
missing/null/malformed/out-of-range -> — / UNAVAILABLE
```
