# Scientific limitations

## Demonstrated

- The installed v783-derived population and signed CSR loaded in the resident environment.
- A normalized global illumination command reached the documented visual root population and was followed by persisted LIF spikes.
- Sampled root IDs and compact indices aligned with the canonical mapping.
- Descending readout, PicoClaw coordination, Pinocchio dispatch, body-state persistence and proprioceptive-transducer code were present in the installed runtime.
- Episode/generation/checkpoint identities and many component records were persisted in SQLite.
- CPU cost was measured by stage, and the normalized reserve debited measured CPU use.

## Not demonstrated

- The complete causal loop from external sensory pattern through Desc spikes, nonzero motor command, body change, proprioceptive activity and a later neural frame.
- A useful visual representation: the tested transducer used one global scalar, with no calibrated image, contrast, optic flow or movement field.
- Anatomical correspondence between sorted-descending-index motor contrasts and actual motor units.
- Physical meaning of the eight graph-only body edges, validated contacts, a supported ground plane, or whole-sigil displacement.
- Nonzero proprioceptive target activity, energy assimilation, biological metabolism, learning, cognition, sentience or biological equivalence.
- Restoration from the final 88,000-tick state. The stored causal cursor ended at 87,800; the later cycle lacked an atomic confirmation record.
- A final live public LETHE display; the public endpoint returned 502 during the service failure.

## Model and infrastructure limits

The VM exposed 964 MiB RAM and no swap in a 1-GB class host, with about 1.2 GB disk free at the incident capture. Tick progression was extremely slow in the measured 30-minute observation. The dense population arrays and directed CSR propagation imposed most of the measured CPU cost. This archive did not optimize or change the implementation.

The LIF model is a simplified computational model. The connectome is a structural graph with estimated signs, not a complete physiological model of cell types, neurotransmission, conductance, muscles or body mechanics. The body is a synthetic sigil. The synthetic input scales and the selected cell mappings were instrumental and not calibrated against a physical animal or sensor.

## Open scientific questions retained as questions

- What structured sensory stimulus and calibration would produce a scientifically interpretable response in this implementation?
- Do the runtime's direct and indirect paths support an appropriate descending response under such input?
- Which body edges, if any, have independently justified physical constraints?
- How should a nonzero proprioceptive signal be defined in units and linked to later neural processing?
- What runtime and checkpoint protocol would provide causal durability without blocking the live writer?

These are unresolved questions, not accepted defects to repair. The project is ended; they do not authorize further experiments.
