- E01: PARTIAL; Pinocchio 4.1.0, ARM64/Python 3.12, synthetic URDF body with four DoF.
- E01: ZeroClaw/Pinocchio unavailability tests and limit-rejection tests passed in fail-closed mode.
- E01: coordination remained in an external experimental adapter; it was not promoted into ZeroClaw.
- Resident loop: evidence existed of continuous brain, world, and body operation, but the memory-stability window was not completed before the host was lost.
- MAX: unavailability was host-wide; the exact cause was not proven.

The roadmap later supplied records a more advanced product stage on 2026-09-15, including E01-D PASS, E01-N PARTIAL, E03 PASS, H01 PASS_WITH_RESEARCH_BLOCKER, and persistent resident trajectory. These are historical MAX results and do not substitute for present verification, which was blocked by loss of access to the VM.

The later addendum records that `ENERGY_TO_BODY_CAPACITY` was implemented, that the living-capacity floor was corrected to `0.01`, that locomotion semantics were separated from geometric body movement, and that the mobile Cave surface was implemented. It also preserves the limits: real contact/consumption were not observed, the linear-memory-growth fix remained `UNVERIFIED`, and resident continuity was externally interrupted.

The later canonical status was `PROJECT_STATUS=RETIRED_FROZEN`, `RESIDENT_RUNTIME_STATUS=INTERRUPTED_EXTERNALLY`, and `CURRENT_RESUMPTION_PLAN=NONE`. Oracle suspension, loss of access, and possible data deletion remain distinct claims: `RESOURCE_DELETION=UNPROVEN` and `DATA_PRESERVATION=UNKNOWN`. The addendum is not proof that the runtime is operational today.

As of 2026-09-21, Flynn has been reopened as a non-commercial research and open-knowledge initiative seeking appropriate persistent infrastructure. This reopening does not retroactively restore the original resident's temporal continuity.
